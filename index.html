How to play

A/D move · Space double jump · J light combo · K heavy strike · U technique · Q/E cycle copied powers · R Rika command · G ultimate · H full Rika manifestation · Shift dash.

Hardcore campaign: six worlds, six levels each. Enemy roles have different tactics: stalkers pursue and flank, bruisers break guard, ranged fighters keep distance, duelists dodge predictable attacks, and supports buff allies. Bosses gain phase changes. Yuta permanently copies a technique from each enemy category on the first defeat; repeated defeats improve mastery. Six techniques can be equipped at a time.

Power-ups & progress

Yuta: sword combos, copied cursed techniques, Domain Expansion and Rika. Rika attacks automatically and strongly guards Yuta, with cooldown-based protection. Fill her bond gauge to activate six seconds of full manifestation. Other playable fighters have distinct special moves and ultimates. One support slot is saved for the party; Rika is Yuta-exclusive.
