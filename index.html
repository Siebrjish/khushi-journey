How to play

←/→ move · Space jump · J Cleave · K Dismantle · L domain expansion · Shift dash.

Each level features ONE rival sorcerer: Gojo, Yuji, Nanami, Mei Mei, or Kakuganji-inspired. Every rival has a unique technique, and jumps between platforms to pursue you. Collect gold stars, grab power-ups, touch glowing checkpoints, and reach the portal. Every fourth level ends with a boss. Moving platforms have absolutely no respect for your schedule.

Power-ups & progress

⚔ Starblade: slash nearby enemies and damage bosses. ✦ Dash: burst forward. ◆ Shield: temporary protection. ↑ Moon boots: higher jumps. Checkpoints save your return point. Your best score and unlocked levels save on this device.
